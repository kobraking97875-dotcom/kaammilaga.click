Job Interview Questions for Freshers

Prepare realistic answers for basic interviews in Hindi or English.

Tell me about yourself

Keep it short: education, relevant skill, city, and why the role interests you. Example: “I completed my studies and I am looking for an entry-level warehouse role where I can learn and work responsibly.”

Why should we hire you?

Give evidence from actual experience or willingness to learn; do not invent certifications.

What salary do you expect?

Ask for the job’s gross pay, in-hand pay, PF/ESI deductions, OT terms, weekly offs and payment date before accepting.

What should you ask the employer?

Request job location, shifts, offer letter, reporting person, salary breakup and company identity.
