Partnership Policies
Application process ko transparent aur applicants ke liye safe banane ke liye basic policies.

 
🔒 Privacy Policy
Naam, age, email, city, qualification aur uploaded proof names ka use owner verification aur future contact ke liye hoga. Form submit hone par application record Supabase database mein aur proofs private Storage bucket mein save honge. Sirf authorized owner verification kar sakta hai.

 
🛡️ Safety & Anti-Fraud
Never share passwords, OTP, UPI PIN or full identity details in unsolicited chats. Upload only masked Aadhaar through the private application form. Kisi ko bina verification ke payment na karein. Forged credentials aur misuse par lawful action possible hai.

 
📜 Partnership Terms
Rights, roles, compensation/commission, fee and refund terms final signed document mein clear honge. Enquiry submission alone does not create legal rights or obligations of partnership.

 
🎧 Customer Support
Partnership enquiries, correction requests, privacy concerns aur suspected fraud reports ke liye founder/support email par contact karein.
