Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

KaamMilega ("we", "us", or "our") provides a platform that connects employers, contractors and workers for job and hiring opportunities.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, and the choices available to users.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use KaamMilega, we may collect information such as your mobile number used for account verification, name, city, skills, job details, employer or contractor information, contact details supplied in job postings, profile photos, and information you voluntarily submit through support or hiring forms.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information to create and manage accounts, display relevant job listings and profiles, connect employers and workers, provide customer support, process paid featured-job requests, prevent abuse, maintain platform security, and improve the service.

3. Authentication and Account Security

KaamMilega uses mobile verification and account-access controls. Users should never share an OTP, password, access token, or payment credentials with another person.

4. Job Listings and Public Information

Information intentionally submitted for a public job listing or public profile, such as a name, city, skills, job title or job description, may be visible to other users where required for the platform's hiring features. Do not publish sensitive personal information in a public job description.

5. Payments

Paid featured-job services may use PayU or another configured payment provider. Payment credentials should be entered only on the payment provider's secure checkout page. KaamMilega does not ask users to send card PINs, OTPs or payment passwords through WhatsApp or support chat.

6. Advertising

KaamMilega may display advertising through Google advertising services, including Google AdMob in the mobile application. Advertising providers may use information permitted by their policies to provide, measure and personalize advertising. Users can review Google's applicable privacy and advertising controls for more information.

7. Third-Party Services

KaamMilega may rely on third-party services for authentication, database hosting, storage, payments, messaging and advertising. These providers process information according to their own applicable terms and privacy policies.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. However, no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the service, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and keep appropriate business records. If you want to request deletion or correction of information, contact KaamMilega support at kaammilaga.official@gmail.com.

10. Children's Privacy

KaamMilega is intended for users who can legally use employment and hiring services. We do not knowingly request personal information from children for employment purposes.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the service, legal requirements, or data practices change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new update date.

12. Contact

For privacy or account questions, contact KaamMilega Customer Support by emailing kaammilaga.official@gmail.com or through the support option provided on the website.
