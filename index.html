ABOUT KAAMMILEGA

What KaamMilega does

KaamMilega is an independent employment marketplace designed to make job discovery and worker hiring easier for people in India. The platform brings job seekers, employers and contractors together in one place. A worker can search by skill and city, review the information supplied by an employer, and contact the relevant listing. An employer can publish a vacancy, describe the required skill, location, job type and salary, and receive enquiries from interested workers.

The platform also supports a hiring-desk workflow for situations where a company or contractor needs several workers. Companies can submit the type of worker required, the number of people needed, work location, salary or rate, joining date and other practical details. KaamMilega can then use the submitted requirement for coordination and follow-up. A company requirement shown on the website is not a promise of employment; the employer remains responsible for the final hiring decision and for confirming the vacancy.

KaamMilega is not a government department, recruitment regulator or employer of every vacancy displayed on the site. Job information can change, so users should confirm the employer, role, location, salary, working conditions and joining details before accepting an offer or travelling for an interview.

HOW IT WORKS

A clear process for job seekers and employers

1. Search or post

Workers can search the latest public jobs using a skill or job title and a city. Employers can use the Post a Job form to provide the vacancy title, category, location, salary, job type, description and contact number.

2. Review details

Read the complete job description before contacting an employer. Check the location, salary or rate, experience requirement, shift, documents and any other conditions that matter to you.

3. Contact and confirm

Use the contact option or KaamMilega support where available. Confirm that the vacancy is still open and that the person contacting you is connected to the stated employer or hiring requirement.

For employers needing multiple workers, the Company Hiring Requirements area provides a structured way to record the company name, number of members required, worker type, salary or rate, location, joining date and additional instructions. The owner panel is used to update or complete these requirements. Completed requirements are removed from the public list so that old demand is less likely to be mistaken for a current vacancy.

FOR WORKERS

Using KaamMilega to find work

Start by searching for your skill, such as MIG Welder, Electrician, Helper, Delivery Executive, Supervisor or another role. Add your preferred city to narrow the results. Open a listing and read the description carefully rather than relying only on the job title.

Keep your profile useful

Add your name, city and relevant skills to your profile. Accurate information helps employers understand what type of work you are looking for.

Confirm before travelling

Vacancies may close or change. Confirm the employer, interview location, joining date, salary and work conditions before spending money on travel or accommodation.

Never pay for an offer

Be cautious if someone asks for money, OTPs, passwords or payment credentials in exchange for a job. Report suspicious requests to KaamMilega support and independently verify the employer.

FOR EMPLOYERS & CONTRACTORS

Publishing a useful vacancy

A good job listing gives workers enough information to decide whether the opportunity matches their experience and location. Use a specific job title, explain the required skill, state the work location, describe the job type and provide the salary or rate when possible. The description can include experience, shift timing, accommodation, transport, documents, interview details and joining expectations.

If you need several workers, use the urgent hiring or company requirement workflow. State the exact number of workers required and the worker category. This helps the KaamMilega team understand the request and reduces unnecessary back-and-forth.

Employers are responsible for the accuracy of information they submit and for complying with applicable employment, wage, workplace safety and other legal requirements. KaamMilega provides a marketplace and communication workflow; it does not make the final hiring decision on behalf of an employer.

TRUST & SAFETY

Important checks before a job or payment

Verify the opportunity

Confirm the company name, job location, role, salary and joining details directly with the employer. Public vacancy information can become outdated when a position is filled.

Protect account information

Never share your login OTP, password, payment PIN or private account credentials with another person. KaamMilega support does not need your OTP or password to provide normal assistance.

Understand payments

Job posting after the free trial costs ₹299 for a 30-day listing period after owner approval. Payment does not guarantee hiring, applications or employment.

For any suspicious communication, stop the conversation and contact KaamMilega through the support email shown on this website. Do not send money merely because a person claims to represent a company or KaamMilega.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

KaamMilega FAQ

Is KaamMilega a government job website?

No. KaamMilega is an independent employment marketplace. It is not a government department or government agency. Users should verify any government-job claim through the relevant official government source.

Can I post a job for free?

Every registered employer gets one free job post. After that, each job post costs ₹299 and is listed for 30 days after owner approval. Razorpay payment is required before a paid post is submitted.

Does KaamMilega guarantee a job?

No. A listing or support request does not guarantee employment. The employer decides whether to interview or hire a candidate, and the worker should confirm the final terms directly with the employer.

How can a company request multiple workers?

Use Need Workers Urgently or the Company Hiring Requirements workflow. Provide the company name, number of workers, worker type, location, salary or rate and other relevant requirements so the request can be coordinated accurately.

How can I contact support?

Email kaammilaga.official@gmail.com for job posting, urgent hiring requirements, platform questions and general assistance.

PRIVACY

Privacy information

KaamMilega may receive information that users choose to submit through account, profile, job-posting, hiring-requirement and support forms. This can include a name, mobile number, city, skills, job details and contact information. The information is used to operate the requested marketplace feature, communicate about a submitted requirement and provide support.

Do not submit passwords, OTPs, payment PINs or other secrets in public job descriptions or support forms. Account authentication is handled through the website's authentication service. Payment processing for paid promotion is handled through the configured payment provider; users should review the payment provider's own terms and privacy information when completing a transaction.

Users can contact KaamMilega support regarding questions about information submitted through the website. The website may also use cookies or browser storage needed for login and basic functionality, and advertising services may use their own technologies subject to their applicable policies.

TERMS & USE

Basic website terms

Users should submit truthful, lawful and relevant information. Do not post fraudulent vacancies, misleading salary claims, abusive content, illegal employment requests or another person's private information without permission.

Job listings and company requirements may be edited, completed or removed when they are no longer current or when they do not meet the site's requirements. KaamMilega may display third-party company names or links for job-search assistance, but the presence of a company name does not by itself mean that KaamMilega is that company's employer or official representative.

Before accepting a job, workers should independently confirm the employer and employment terms. Employers should verify candidate information and make their own hiring decisions in accordance with applicable law.
