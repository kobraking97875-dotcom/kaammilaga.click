How to Write a Simple Fresher Resume

Use your accurate education, useful skills and contact details. No fake work experience.

Start with a clear title

Write your full name, city, reachable phone number and professional email. Do not put full Aadhaar or PAN details in your CV.

Show relevant abilities

For a helper or factory role, list real skills such as packing, assembly assistance, safety awareness, and ability to work shifts—but only if true.

Keep work history truthful

If you are a fresher, write “Fresher” and include relevant training, projects or volunteering you actually completed.

Save a readable PDF

Use simple headings and one or two pages. Review spelling and contact details before sending.
