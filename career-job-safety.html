How to Identify Fake Job Offers

Protect your money, identity documents and personal information during recruitment.

Never pay a guaranteed-job fee

Be suspicious of interview fees, compulsory deposits or instant-selection offers. KaamMilega does not charge job seekers for applying.

Check the employer

Verify the company website, address, official telephone number and recruiter identity independently.

Protect identity documents

Do not send OTPs, passwords, bank PINs or full Aadhaar/PAN images to unknown recruiters. Share only necessary details through trusted channels.

Report suspicious listings

Keep screenshots and contact information. Report questionable posts to the website team and relevant authorities if fraud is suspected.
