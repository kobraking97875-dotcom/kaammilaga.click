Understanding Salary, Overtime and Deductions

Read your wage breakup before joining a company.

Gross versus in-hand salary

Gross pay is before deductions. In-hand pay can be lower after applicable employee contributions, advances and other valid deductions.

Overtime calculation

Ask whether OT is calculated on basic wages or another base, and what legal rate applies. A company’s stated single-rate policy is not automatically proof of compliance.

PF and ESI

Actual PF/ESI eligibility and contribution bases depend on applicable rules. Enter the basis and rates from your payslip or official payroll information.

Keep records

Preserve attendance, wage slips, employment agreements and bank statements; use them to cross-check your calculator results.
